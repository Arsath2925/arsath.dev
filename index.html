Case Study: Where Should the Next Rupee Go?

Sample project with illustrative data

A lead-generation channel analysis. I compare four channels on cost and quality, then recommend how to shift budget.

Problem

One campaign ran on four channels. Instagram brought the most leads, so it looked like the winner. Did it really pay off?

Method

Combine spend, leads, qualified leads and customers per channel. Clean and pivot in Excel, join tables in SQL, then compare cost per lead against cost per customer.

Answer

Volume was misleading. Pick a metric below to see how the ranking changes.

Recommendation

Move about a third of the Instagram budget to email and search, keep LinkedIn for high-intent leads, and re-test for four weeks before changing anything else. Track cost per customer, not just lead count.

The numbers are made up to show my method. Spend is in rupees.
